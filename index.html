Yicheng Zhang

Undergraduate Student, Tsinghua University · Intern, Z.ai

I am an undergraduate student in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University, and currently an intern at Z.ai.

I study how foundation models can tackle harder, longer-horizon problems — and how to measure that honestly. My work spans post-training and evaluation for frontier long-horizon tasks, such as AI agents doing ML research; the mechanisms of multimodal encoding, i.e. how vision-language models read images and text together; and efficient attention and ML systems.

02 Multimodal Encoding Mechanisms How foundation models see

  1. Preprint First author

    Replay Before You Reply: Second-Look Prompting for Unified Multimodal Encoding

    Yicheng Zhang, Xun Zhang, Jingyuan Ma, Tianyu Yu, Yuan Yao

    Preprint, 2026

    Under causal attention, whichever modality comes first is encoded blind to the other. Replaying the image–question pair lets every token see the full input — training-free. The second image acts as a prefill scratchpad: even a blank one can improve reasoning.

    • 12 / 12 VLMs improve
    • +5.6 on MATH-Vision (Gemini 3 Flash)
    • ≤ 0.16 s avg. latency change
    Website
  2. ICML 2026

    Deep Pre-Alignment for VLMs

    Tianyu Yu, Kechen Fang, Zihao Wan, Kaidong Zhang, Yicheng Zhang, Jun Song, Bo Zheng, Yuan Yao

    International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML), 2026

    Swaps the ViT encoder for a small VLM as the perceiver, so visual features reach the LLM already aligned with its text space — instead of spending the LLM's early layers on alignment.

    • +1.9 @ 4B · +3.0 @ 32B
    • −32.9% language forgetting
    arXiv Code Models
