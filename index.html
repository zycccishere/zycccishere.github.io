About Me

I am an undergraduate student in Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University (since 2023), and currently a post-training research intern at Z.ai.

Research Interests

I want foundation models to solve harder, longer-horizon problems — and to measure that honestly. My research currently focuses on:

  • Frontier Long-Horizon Tasks: Post-Training & Evaluation. Can agents do open-ended research work, such as inventing ML methods that generalize and scale? How do we evaluate that rigorously, and train for it? Related work: MLS-Bench
  • Multimodal Encoding Mechanisms. How do vision-language models read an image and a question together under causal attention, and how can input presentation or architecture make that encoding unified? Related work: Replay Before You Reply, Deep Pre-Alignment
  • Efficient Attention & ML Systems. Sparse attention and high-performance GPU kernels for long contexts. Related work: Efficient Attention Survey

Multimodal Encoding Mechanisms · how VLMs read images and text together

Preprint

Replay Before You Reply: Second-Look Prompting for Unified Multimodal Encoding

Yicheng Zhang, Xun Zhang, Jingyuan Ma, Tianyu Yu, Yuan Yao

Preprint, 2026.

PDFProject Page

Under causal attention, whichever modality comes first is encoded blind to the other. Replaying the image–question pair fixes this without training: all 12 VLMs improve, up to +5.6 on MATH-Vision (Gemini 3 Flash). The replayed image acts as a prefill scratchpad — even a blank one helps.

ICML

Deep Pre-Alignment for VLMs

Tianyu Yu, Kechen Fang, Zihao Wan, Kaidong Zhang, Yicheng Zhang, Jun Song, Bo Zheng, Yuan Yao

ICML 2026.

arXivCodeHugging Face

A small VLM replaces the ViT encoder as the perceiver, so visual features reach the LLM already aligned with its text space: +1.9 at 4B, +3.0 at 32B, and 32.9% less language forgetting.
