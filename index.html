About

I am an undergraduate student in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University. I am interested in making large models faster and cheaper, at both the architecture and the systems level, and in AI systems that can help build better AI.

  • Efficient Model Architecture — sparse attention, quantization
  • Machine Learning Systems — CUDA / Triton kernel optimization
  • Recursive Self-Improvement — AI systems that build better AI
